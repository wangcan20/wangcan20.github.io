About

I'm a PhD student in Biostatistics at Johns Hopkins University. I'm currently working with Dr. Yiqun Chen and Dr. Mei-Cheng Wang.

I am broadly interested in developing uncertainty-aware and assumption-lean inference frameworks that integrate statistical principles with ML/AI tools, with a focus on reliability in high-stakes medical applications.
